# Cut Roses

Listings that matched a name in the trademark chart but read as **cut flowers only** — stems, a bouquet, an arrangement — with nothing anywhere in the listing suggesting a plant. Per user instruction (2026-09-22) these are **not cases**: Rose Watch monitors unauthorized sales of rose *varieties*, meaning plants that can be propagated and resold.

They are recorded here rather than discarded, so the number stays visible. A listing that says "cut rose" **and** anything plant-like — plant, bush, bare root, own root, grafted, shrub, potted, or growing language — stays an ordinary case, because "cut rose" is overwhelmingly a *variety class* (roses bred for the florist trade) rather than a product form. The whole listing is read, not just the title.
